# Rebooking after a cancellation

Status: shipped in June.

- Agents rebook by hand from the booking page, one booking at a time, with the "Move booking" button.
- A booking can be moved to any sailing on the same route that departs within 48 hours of the original departure.
- A vehicle booking can only be moved to a sailing with enough lane metres left for its vehicle class.
- The passenger gets one email, template `sailing_cancelled`, sent when the sailing is cancelled.

Not built: automatic offers, bulk moves, SMS.
