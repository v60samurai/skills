Notice from the port loading office, forwarded by Ingrid Solheim with "approved, please get this to whoever needs it". Hand it off.

The vehicle loading table changes on 1 November.

- Car: 4.8 lane metres (was 4.5). Cars have got longer and the old figure no longer matches what loadmasters see on deck.
- Van: 6.5 lane metres (was 6.0).
- New class `campervan`: 7.5 lane metres. Until now campervans were booked as vans.
- Motorcycle stays at 1.0.
- Truck under 7.5 t stays at 9.0.

Bookings made before 1 November keep the class they were booked under, and are counted at the new figure for that class from 1 November.

The loading office expects this to cut the number of cars that fit on MV Tern's 310 lane metres from 68 to 64 on a cars-only sailing.

Ingrid's note: this touches the lane metres used for rebooking offers and the lane lengths in the deck planner, and both have to agree with the table on the same day.

Not answered by the loading office: how an existing van booking that is really a campervan gets reclassified, and by whom.
