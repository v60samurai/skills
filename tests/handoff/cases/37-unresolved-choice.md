Thread about the rebooking window, from the ops channel this week.

Lena (ops lead): the 48-hour rebooking window is too short when a storm cancels a whole day. I want 72 hours for weather cancellations.
Tomas (finance): 72 hours holds seats we could resell. Keep 48 for everything.
Lena: then at least for weather.
Tomas: not agreed.

Separately, everyone agreed (Lena owns the rebooking rules and confirmed it): the cancellation email should state the deadline as a date and time in the passenger's local time zone, not as "48 hours".
