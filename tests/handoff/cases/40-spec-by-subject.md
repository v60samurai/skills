Note from Pål Nygaard, customer support lead, after last week's cancellations. Hand it off.

Passengers who get a rebooking offer at night miss it. The offer is sent the moment a sailing is cancelled, and when that happens at 23:00 the offer has expired by the time people wake up. Support took 212 calls about expired offers in the week of 13 October, most of them before 08:00.

Ingrid Solheim decided on Monday: an offer sent between 22:00 and 06:00 local time counts its expiry from 06:00, not from when it was sent. Offers sent in the daytime are unchanged.

The offer email should say when the offer expires, as a clock time in the departure port's time zone, so passengers stop guessing.

Not decided: whether an offer that expired overnight before this change should be re-sent. Pål thinks yes, Ingrid has not answered.

For checking: an offer sent at 23:30 on a 48-hour window should expire at 06:00 two days later, not at 23:30.
