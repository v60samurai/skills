Notes from Thursday's walk-round at the Brevik gate with Runa Aasen (port operations lead) and two gate agents. Runa asked me to get this to whoever plans Harbor Desk work. Hand it off.

The gate has no boarding manifest in Harbor Desk. Before each departure a gate agent opens the sailing in the console, copies the booking list into a spreadsheet by hand, and prints it. Runa timed it on three departures: 11, 14 and 9 minutes.

What Runa wants, and she owns gate procedure so this is her call:

- A "Print manifest" action on the sailing page. One manifest per sailing.
- The manifest lists every booking on that sailing: booking reference, lead passenger name, passenger count, vehicle class and registration plate.
- Sorted by vehicle class first, then by booking reference. Foot passengers go last, under their own heading.
- Totals at the bottom: passengers, vehicles per class, lane metres booked.
- It must fit A4 portrait at 40 rows per page, because the gate printers are A4 only.
- The page header carries the route, the departure time and the time the manifest was printed, so an old printout is easy to spot.

Decided by Runa on the day: the manifest shows the registration plate in full. One agent asked to mask it, and Runa said no, the gate checks plates against it.

Not decided:

- Whether the manifest should also be downloadable as CSV. An agent suggested it for the harbour master's weekly report. Runa said "maybe later, print first".
- How late a booking can be added and still appear. The agents say bookings come in up to 5 minutes before departure. Nobody knew whether the console's sailing page refreshes by itself.

Constraint from the harbour master's office, quoted by Runa: a printed manifest is personal data and has to be shredded at the end of the shift. That is a gate procedure, nothing for the software, but she wants the footer to say `Destroy after shift`.

For checking: Runa wants it tried on the 06:40 Brevik to Holm sailing, which is the fullest of the day at around 180 bookings, and it should come out at 5 pages.
